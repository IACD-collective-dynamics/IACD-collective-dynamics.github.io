About

Traffic, crowds, swarms.

The International Association for Collective Dynamics (IACD) is an interdisciplinary scientific association bringing together researchers interested in the quantitative understanding of traffic and transport systems, broadly defined. Its scope ranges from vehicular traffic and pedestrian and crowd dynamics to the collective motion of animal groups and swarms, and the flow of granular materials.

The IACD brings together members from a wide range of disciplines, including physics, mathematics, engineering, computational science, and social psychology.

A central aim of the IACD is to foster scientific exchange and interdisciplinary collaboration among its members. To this end, the association organizes and supports scientific activities such as conferences and workshops.
