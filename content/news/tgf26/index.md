---

The 16th Traffic and Granular Flow conference (TGF26) took place from 16 to 19 June 2026 at the University of Bristol, UK. The programme had five keynote lectures, more than 60 talks and close to 50 posters.

On 18 June, the TGF steering committee extended its meeting into the founding meeting of the International Association for Collective Dynamics. 24 members founded the association, adopted its statutes and elected the first Executive Board: Andreas Schadschneider (Chair), Jun Zhang (Deputy Chair) and Mohcine Chraibi (Treasurer). The IACD was entered in the Vereinsregister of the Amtsgericht Düren on 29 July 2026.

The next TGF will be held in Wuppertal, Germany, from 6 to 9 June 2028, under the umbrella of the IACD. See [TGF28]({{< relref "/tgf28" >}}).

Nikolai Bode has written a full report on the conference, with the keynotes and the social programme, on [pedestriandynamics.org](https://pedestriandynamics.org/academic_update/tgf26/).
