## 2. Visiting this website

This website is hosted on **GitHub Pages**, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P.
Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, GitHub
processes the technical data your browser sends, in particular your IP address, the
date and time of access, the page requested, and your browser and operating system,
in order to deliver the website and keep it secure. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR
(our legitimate interest in a secure, reliably available website).

GitHub may process this data in the USA. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the
EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). Further information:
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

This website does **not** use cookies, analytics or tracking. Fonts and the site
search are served from this website itself; no data is sent to Google or other
third parties for that. The light/dark display preference may be stored locally in
your browser and is never transmitted to us.

## 3. Contacting us and applying for membership

If you email us, for example to apply for membership, we process the data you send
(such as name, email address, affiliation, membership category) to handle your
request. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (membership and pre-contractual steps) or
Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (answering general enquiries).

## 4. Members

For members we keep a member register (name, email address, affiliation, country,
membership category, member number, dates of joining and payment). We use this data
only to manage the membership, collect fees, invite members to the General Assembly
and inform them about the association's activities. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR
(membership under the statutes). Payment records are kept as required by German tax
and commercial law (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), generally for up to ten years.

## 5. Payments

**Bank transfer:** our account is held with Deutsche Skatbank (VR-Bank Altenburger
Land eG), Germany. The bank processes the data of your transfer.

**Online payment:** payments by card or other online methods are processed by
**Stripe** (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Dublin 2,
Ireland). When you use a payment link, you enter your payment data directly with
Stripe; we do not receive your full card details. Stripe may transfer data to the
USA and is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Further information:
[Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

## 6. LinkedIn

Our website links to our LinkedIn page. The link is a plain link: no data is sent
to LinkedIn until you click it. When you visit our LinkedIn page, LinkedIn's
privacy policy applies.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure
(Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to
object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21). To exercise these rights,
write to us at the address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The
authority responsible for us is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und
Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW), Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213
Düsseldorf, Germany.
